## "Why not just use Google Classroom? It's free."

Three strongest answers, based **only on what the platform does today**:

1. **It survives exam day.** Quizzes now run on a server-side clock, save every answer as the student types, keep working through a WiFi drop, and submit the saved answers when time runs out (`feat/exam-robustness`, 12 automated tests). Rate limits are per student, not per campus IP. Classroom assumes homework; it has no server timer and no auto-save.
2. **A gradebook that computes marks the way the college does.** Weighted categories, rubrics, per-member grades for group work, and a per-student transcript export (`gradebook.ts`, `transcript.ts`). Classroom gives you points and an export to Sheets.
3. **The college owns the roles and the records.** Deans and course coordinators get oversight views (`routes/oversight.ts`, `analytics.ts`). There is an activity log, a DPDP data-export endpoint, and the database and files can be placed in India (`MIGRATE-SUPABASE.md`, Mumbai).

**Being fair to the free option:** Classroom is free, already works, and handles assignment late flags and resubmission better (job 3). It also emails students reliably, which this platform doesn't by default (job 5). A Head of Department is right to ask "who is on call during exams?", and today the honest answer is nobody (`ARCHITECTURE.md:305`, "Support model ❌").
